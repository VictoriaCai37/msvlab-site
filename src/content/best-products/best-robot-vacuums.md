## What to Look For

1. **Mop washing temperature**: 60°C+ sanitizes; 80°C is hospital-grade
2. **Extending mops**: Reach baseboards/corners round robots miss
3. **Obstacle avoidance**: AI + camera avoids pet waste, cables, toys
4. **Dock size**: Measure your space — RockDock Ultra needs 18.5" width
5. **Annual consumables**: $120-200/year for bags, mops, filters, solution

## Our Testing Methodology

* **Vacuum test**: 100g mixed debris (rice, cereal, pet hair, sand) on each surface
* **Mop test**: Coffee, ketchup, soy sauce, mud stains (dried 2h, 24h)
* **Edge test**: Flour line along baseboards + corner dust
* **Obstacle course**: Cables, toys, shoes, pet waste simulant, floor vents
* **Maintenance log**: Frequency of bin emptying, mop washing, brush cleaning
* **App test**: Map editing, scheduling, zone cleaning, voice, firmware updates

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a robot vacuum AND a stick vacuum?**
A: For most homes, a flagship robot (S8 MaxV Ultra, X2 Omni) handles 90% of cleaning. Keep a stick vac for stairs, upholstery, cars, and deep carpet.

**Q: Are heated mop washes worth the extra cost?**
A: Yes. 60°C+ kills bacteria, prevents mildew smell, removes dried stains better. The $200-300 premium pays off in hygiene and manual cleaning reduction.

**Q: Can robot vacuums handle high-pile carpet?**
A: Most struggle with >1 inch pile. Look for high suction (7,000+ Pa) and carpet boost. For shag/rugs, manual vacuum still needed.
