## The Brush That Changed Everything

Ask any pet owner their #1 robot vacuum annoyance: **cutting hair off the roller brush**. Narwal listened. The **Freo X Ultra** debuts a **conical zero-tangle floating brush** — a single helical ribbon that funnels hair directly into the suction inlet. No bristles, no rubber fins, **zero wrap**. In 4 weeks with two golden retrievers, we found **zero hair** on the brush. Not "less" — **zero**.

## Hygiene Obsessed: 78°C + Electrolyzed Water

The base station heats water to **78°C** for mop washing, then generates **electrolyzed water** (hypochlorous acid) for final rinse and floor mopping. Lab-tested 99.9% kill rate for E. coli, Staphylococcus, and Candida. No chemical residue — safe for babies and pets. The station also dries mops with 40°C hot air.

**DirtSense 2.0** uses optical sensors to measure mop water turbidity. If the water's still dirty after a pass, it re-mops that zone automatically. In our muddy-paw test, it took 3 passes to declare "clean."

## Triangular Mops: Grout Line Warriors

Round pads skip over grout lines. Narwal's **dual triangular pads** (180 RPM, 12N pressure) dig into grout and texture. On our textured porcelain tile, they removed 40% more embedded dirt than round pads. Edge cleaning relies on the triangular tips — decent, but not as thorough as extending arms.

## Vacuuming Performance

8,200 Pa suction + zero-tangle brush = **best pet hair pickup we've tested**. The brush stays clean, so suction never drops. On carpet, the 12mm lift raises mops adequately. Single side brush is a minor weakness — debris within 0.5" of walls sometimes needs a second pass.

|---------|----------|-----------|--------------|
| Hardwood | 99% | 98% | 97% |
| Tile | 98% | 97% | 96% |
| Low-pile carpet | 95% | 92% | 90% |

## The Trade-offs

* **No extending mops** — edges good, not great
* **Basic app** — no 3D map editing, no room-specific suction/mop, no voice assistant
* **Proprietary solution** — electrolyzed water needs Narwal's concentrate (~$20/month)
* **Single side brush** — wall-hugging could be better

## Verdict

If **pet hair** and **hygiene** are your top priorities, the **Freo X Ultra is unmatched**. The zero-tangle brush alone justifies the $1,399 for pet owners. The electrolyzed water disinfection is a bonus for families with young kids.

If you need **extending mops**, **advanced app control**, or **voice commands**, look at Roborock S8 Pro Ultra ($1,199) or Dreame L20 Ultra ($1,299).
