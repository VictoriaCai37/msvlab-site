## Magnetic Switches Go Mainstream

**Hall Effect switches** have been in niche gaming keyboards (Wooting, SteelSeries) for years. The **Keychron Q1 HE** puts them in a **premium 75% aluminum custom keyboard** with **VIA/QMK**, **per-key RGB**, **gasket mount**, and **adjustable actuation (0.1-4.0mm)** — for **$219 assembled**. It's the best value in mechanical keyboards right now.

We tested the **assembled Black Aluminum / HE Linear / Double-shot PBT** model for 6 weeks: gaming (CS2, Valorant, osu!), coding, writing.

## Hall Effect: How It Works & Why It Matters

Traditional MX switches: physical leaf contact → wear, bounce, fixed actuation.

**Hall Effect**: magnet in stem + Hall sensor on PCB → **no contact**, **analog position sensing**.

| Benefit | Explanation |
|---------|-------------|
| **Adjustable Actuation** | Set per-key actuation 0.1-4.0mm (0.1mm steps). Light for gaming (0.5mm), deep for typing (2.5mm). |
| **Rapid Trigger** | Key releases *instantly* when you lift past release point (not fixed reset). Eliminates "stuck" feeling in counter-strafing. |
| **Analog Input** | Keys report 0-100% press depth — enable analog movement in supported games (racing, flight sims). |
| **100M+ Keystrokes** | No physical wear on contacts. Rated 100M+ cycles. |
| **Zero Chatter/Bounce** | Magnetic sensing = no debounce needed. |

## Rapid Trigger in Games: It's Real

In **CS2/Valorant**: counter-strafing (tap A/D to stop instantly) feels sharper. The key releases the *moment* your finger lifts — no waiting for switch reset. In **osu!**: streaming (alternating taps) at 250+ BPM feels easier with 0.5mm actuation + Rapid Trigger.

**Not magic** — skill still matters. But the hardware ceiling is higher.

## VIA/QMK: No Software, Just Browser

Open **Keychron Launcher** (Chrome/Edge only — WebHID) → remap keys, create macros, configure layers, adjust actuation per key, program RGB, set Rapid Trigger curves. **No install, no background service, works on Linux/Mac/Windows.**

Analog settings: actuation point, release point, Rapid Trigger sensitivity, dead zone — all per-key.

## Gaming vs. Typing: Two Keyboards in One

| Profile | Actuation | Rapid Trigger | Use Case |
|---------|-----------|---------------|----------|
| **Gaming** | 0.5-1.0mm | On (0.1mm) | FPS, rhythm, fighting games |
| **Typing** | 2.0-2.5mm | Off | Writing, coding, email |
| **Hybrid** | 1.2mm (alphas), 2.0mm (mods) | On (alphas) | Mixed use |

Switch profiles instantly in Launcher. We settled on 1.2mm alphas + 2.0mm mods + RT on WASD.

## The Catch: HE Switches Only

**Only Keychron HE switches work.** No standard MX, no Gateron KS-20 (different magnet polarity). Keychron sells HE Linear, HE Tactile (clicky coming). Hot-swappable — but only within HE ecosystem.

## Verdict

**Best keyboard under $250.** Hall Effect + Rapid Trigger + adjustable actuation + VIA/QMK + aluminum CNC + gasket mount + per-key RGB = feature set that costs $300+ elsewhere (Wooting 60HE $199 barebone, no VIA, plastic case).

**Buy if**: You want to experience magnetic switches, need Rapid Trigger for gaming, love VIA customization, want a premium 75% board.

**Skip if**: You need wireless, prefer standard MX switch variety, use Firefox/Safari exclusively, or want a simpler "just works" board (Keychron V1/Q1 standard are great).

The Q1 HE makes Hall Effect accessible. Once you try Rapid Trigger, it's hard to go back.
