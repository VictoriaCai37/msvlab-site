## The Square Peg in a Round Hole

For a decade, robot vacuums have been round. Ecovacs asked a simple question: **Why?** Walls are straight. Corners are 90°. A round robot leaves a triangle of uncleaned space in every corner. The **Deebot X2 Omni** answers with a square body that reaches where circles can't — and backs it up with a fully retractable mop, 15mm carpet lift, and the compact OMNI station.

We tested it for five weeks in a 2,400 sq ft home with tile, hardwood, area rugs, and medium-pile carpet.

## Square Design: Does It Actually Work?

**Yes.** The flat sides and 90° corners let the X2 Omni clean baseboards and room corners that round robots miss. In our "dust line" test (flour spread along baseboards), the X2 removed 94% vs. 78% for the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra. The single extending mop arm deploys for edges, adding another 1.2 inches of reach.

The trade-off: the **recessed LiDAR module** adds 0.4 inches of height (3.9" total at the center). It couldn't fit under our IKEA KALLAX shelf (4" clearance) where the Roborock S8 (3.7") slid freely.

## OMNI Station: Compact but Complete

The OMNI station measures 17.3 x 15.7 x 17.3 inches — noticeably shorter and narrower than Roborock's RockDock Ultra. It handles:
* **60°C hot water mop washing** (vs. Roborock's 80°C)
* **Hot air drying** (2–3 hours)
* **Auto dust emptying** into 3 L bag
* **Clean/dirty water tanks** (4 L each)

It's easier to place in tight laundry rooms or kitchen nooks.

## Vacuuming & Mopping Performance

**8,000 Pa suction** with **dual rubber roller brushes** excels at pet hair. On carpet, the 15mm mop lift activates instantly — no damp streaks on rugs. Mopping pressure is adjustable (three levels); the rotating mops spin at 180 RPM. Coffee spill test: 90% removal first pass. Dried ketchup: two passes.

| Surface | Vacuum Pickup | Mop Stain Removal (1 pass) |
|---------|---------------|----------------------------|
| Hardwood | 98% | 92% |
| Tile | 97% | 90% |
| Low-pile carpet | 94% | N/A (mop lifts) |
| Medium-pile carpet | 90% | N/A |

## Obstacle Avoidance: AIVI 3D 2.0

Combines dToF LiDAR with dual RGB cameras and an NPU. It recognized:
* Shoes and slippers
* Cables (USB, power, HDMI)
* Pet waste (tested)
* Furniture legs, floor vents
* Toys >1.5 inches tall

Occasional false positives: it once avoided a dark floor mat as a "cliff." Map alignment drifted slightly after 3 weeks — a quick "remap" fixed it.

## App & Smart Features

Ecovacs Home app covers all basics: multi-map, virtual walls, zone cleaning, schedules, suction/mop per room, voice control ("OK YIKO"). The 3D map view is impressive but less editable than Roborock's. Firmware updates are frequent.

## Verdict

The **Deebot X2 Omni** is the **best square robot vacuum** we've tested — and a genuine alternative to Roborock's dominance. The corner-cleaning advantage is real, the OMNI station is more space-efficient, and $1,499 undercuts the S8 MaxV Ultra by $300.

**Choose X2 Omni if:** Corner cleaning matters, dock space is tight, you want 15mm mop lift.

**Choose Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra if:** You need 80°C sanitation, dual extending mop arms, lower profile for furniture.
