## The Renter's Dream Lock

Most smart locks require removing your deadbolt — a dealbreaker for renters and a hassle for homeowners. The **SwitchBot Lock Pro** clamps over your **existing thumbturn** in 10 minutes with a screwdriver. No drilling, no locksmith, no damaged deposits.

We tested it on 6 different deadbolts (Schlage, Kwikset, Yale, Baldwin, generic) — it fit all but one antique mortise lock.

## Keypad Touch: The Best Keypad We've Used

The optional **Keypad Touch** ($69 standalone, $59 in bundle) is a revelation:
* **Capacitive backlit keys** — visible at night, no mechanical wear
* **Fingerprint sensor** — 0.5s, stores 100 prints, works with wet/dry fingers
* **NFC card support** — issue cards to guests/cleaners
* **IP65 weatherproof** — handles rain, snow, humidity
* **Anti-peep codes** — enter random digits before/after your real code

It looks like a high-end door accessory, not a plastic gadget.

## Matter/Thread: Future-Proof

With the **SwitchBot Hub 2** (Matter bridge), the Lock Pro joins Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, and SmartThings natively. No cloud dependency for local control. Automations like "unlock → lights on → thermostat adjust" run locally.

**Without the Hub**: Bluetooth-only, app control within ~30 ft, no voice/Matter.

## Battery Life: The One Real Compromise

4× CR123A batteries last **~6 months** (vs. 12–18 months for Wi-Fi locks like Yale Assure 2). The app gives 2-week low-battery warnings. CR123As are widely available ($8/4-pack). For a retrofit lock with fingerprint + keypad, it's acceptable.

## Installation Walkthrough

1. Remove existing thumbturn cover (if any)
2. Place Lock Pro over thumbturn, align adapter
3. Tighten two clamping screws with included hex key
4. Calibrate in app (lock/unlock 3x)
5. Pair Keypad Touch (scan QR, enter pairing code)
6. Add fingerprints, codes, NFC cards

Total time: **9 minutes 40 seconds** on a standard Schlage.

## Verdict

**Buy the bundle (Lock Pro + Keypad Touch + Hub 2) for ~$290.** You get fingerprint, keypad, NFC, Matter, and remote access — the complete package. If you already have a Matter hub, skip the Hub 2.

The only reasons *not* to buy: you need 12+ month battery life, or your deadbolt is non-standard. For everyone else, this is the smart lock to get.
