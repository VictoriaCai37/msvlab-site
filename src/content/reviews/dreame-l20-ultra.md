## The Value Flagship Has Arrived

Dreame has been nipping at Roborock's heels for years. The **L20 Ultra** is their first true flagship killer: **70°C hot water mop washing**, **MopExtend edge cleaning**, **7,000 Pa suction**, and **3D structured light navigation** — all for **$1,299**. That's $500 less than the Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra and $200 less than Ecovacs X2 Omni.

Four weeks of testing in a 2,600 sq ft home with tile, hardwood, and medium-pile carpet.

## MopExtend: One Arm, But It Works

Unlike Roborock's dual extending arms, Dreame deploys **only the right mop** for edges. In practice, this covers ~85% of the edge-cleaning benefit. The mop extends 1.3 inches, reaching baseboards and corner junctions. It's not as thorough as dual arms, but for a $500 savings, it's a fair trade.

The **70°C wash** is hotter than Ecovacs' 60°C and effectively sanitizes mops. Hot air drying takes 2–3 hours. No mildew smell detected after 4 weeks.

## Vacuuming: Strong for the Price

7,000 Pa with **dual rubber roller brushes** (no bristles = less hair tangling). Pet hair pickup on carpet matched the Roborock S8 Pro Ultra in our tests. The **10.5mm mop lift** clears most area rugs, though thick shag carpets (>15mm) still get damp edges.

| Surface | Pickup | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Hardwood | 98% | Excellent |
| Tile | 97% | Excellent |
| Low-pile carpet | 93% | Good |
| Medium-pile carpet | 88% | Decent |

## Navigation & Obstacle Avoidance

**LiDAR + 3D structured light** (no RGB camera). It maps accurately and avoids furniture legs, cables >3mm, and toys >2 inches. It struggles with:
* Very low obstacles (&lt;1 inch) — may push them
* Pet waste — no camera = no visual recognition
* Dark furniture in dim rooms — structured light needs some ambient light

For a camera-free system, it's very good. If you need pet waste avoidance, spend more on Roborock/Ecovacs.

## App Experience

Dreamehome app has all essential features: multi-map, virtual walls, zone cleaning, room-specific suction/mop, schedules, firmware updates. Quirks: some Chinese-to-English translations are clunky ("MopExtend" labeled as "Mop Extend" in one menu, "Edge Clean" in another). No built-in voice assistant.

**Buy it.** Your floors will thank you, and your wallet will too.
