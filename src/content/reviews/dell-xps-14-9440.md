## The Windows Answer to MacBook Air (With a Twist)

Dell took the XPS 13 formula — machined aluminum, infinity-edge display, premium everything — and **stretched it to 14.5 inches** for a **3.2K 120 Hz OLED** and **optional RTX 4050**. The result: the **most compelling 14-inch creator laptop** since Apple went Apple Silicon.

We tested the **Core Ultra 7 155H / RTX 4050 / 32 GB / 1 TB / 3.2K OLED touch** config ($2,299) for 5 weeks of video editing, photo work, and daily driving.

## Display: The Star of the Show

**3.2K (3200×2000) 120 Hz OLED** — 100% DCI-P3, 400 nits HDR (500 nits peak), 0.2 ms response, touch optional. It's **stunning**. Perfect blacks make video timelines pop; 120 Hz makes scrubbing buttery. Color accuracy out of box: ΔE < 1 (CalMAN verified). HDR content (Netflix, YouTube HDR) looks reference-grade.

Trade-off: **OLED burns battery**. Expect 6-7 hrs real-world vs. 10+ on LCD models.

## Performance: Core Ultra + RTX 4050 = Creator Cred

**Core Ultra 7 155H** (16-core: 6P+8E+2LPE) + **RTX 4050 6 GB (50W)** + **Intel Arc iGPU** + **NPU (11 TOPS)**.

| Workload | Time/Score | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Cinebench 2024 Multi | 785 | Strong for 14" |
| PugetBench Premiere Pro | 7,850 | RTX 4050 active |
| Blender Monster (GPU) | 420 pts | CUDA + OptiX |
| Gaming (Cyberpunk 1080p High) | 68 fps | DLSS 3.5 Quality |
| NPU (Procyon AI) | 1,120 | Good for local AI |

**Thermals**: Dual fans, vapor chamber. Under sustained load: CPU 92°C, GPU 78°C. Fans hit 48 dB — audible but not jet-engine. **Coil whine** on some units (including ours) at low GPU loads.

## Keyboard & Touchpad: Love It or Hate It

**Zero-lattice keyboard** (no gaps between keys) + **seamless haptic touchpad** (no physical buttons, no visible line). Typing feel: crisp, 1mm travel, but zero tactile edges between keys = more typos initially. Touchpad: precise, customizable haptics, but **no physical click feedback** takes weeks to internalize.

**Verdict**: Excellent for some, dealbreaker for others. Try before buying.

## Ports: Dongle City

2× Thunderbolt 4, 1× USB-C 3.2 Gen 2, microSD, 3.5 mm. **No HDMI, no USB-A**. For creators carrying cameras, drives, monitors — you **need a hub**. Dell includes a USB-C to USB-A/HDMI dongle in box.

## Battery: The OLED Tax

| Scenario | Battery Life |
|----------|--------------|
| Light web/office (50% brightness) | 7h 45m |
| Video playback (Netflix, 50%) | 8h 30m |
| Premiere Pro timeline scrubbing | 3h 15m |
| Gaming (RTX 4050) | 1h 40m |

Charge: 60W adapter (base) / 100W (dGPU models). 0-80% in 55 min.

## Verdict

**Buy XPS 14 if**: You need Windows, OLED color accuracy, discrete GPU for creative apps, and premium build.

**Skip if**: Battery life matters, you hate dongles, you want a traditional keyboard/touchpad, or macOS works for you (MacBook Pro 14 M3 is better in almost every way for $300 less).

The XPS 14 is a **specialized tool** — and for its niche, it's excellent.
