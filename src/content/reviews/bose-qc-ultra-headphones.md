## Comfort King Gets a Spatial Upgrade

Bose has always owned **comfort**. The **QC Ultra** keeps that throne — **plush protein leather, minimal clamping force, 254g** — and adds **Immersive Audio** (Bose's spatial audio with head tracking) plus **aptX Adaptive**. The result: the most comfortable flagship with legitimate spatial audio that works on *any* device, any content.

We tested Lunar Blue for 8 weeks: flights, office, gaming, movies, music.

## Comfort: The Benchmark

**Earcup depth**: 28mm (XM5: 22mm) — ears float, never touch driver.
**Clamping force**: ~2.8N (XM5: ~3.5N) — secure but feather-light.
**Weight distribution**: Headband padding + earcup balance = no hot spots.

**10-hour flights**: zero fatigue. **Glasses**: zero temple pressure. **Sleeping on plane**: earcups rotate flat, rest on shoulder comfortably.

This is the **only headphone we'd recommend for 12+ hour sessions**.

## ANC: Quiet Mode = XM5 Peer, Beats It on Voices

**Quiet mode** (max ANC): matches XM5 on low-frequency (engine, HVAC). **Beats XM5 on mid/high** (voices, announcements, keyboard clatter) — Bose's digital ANC algorithms are sharper on transients.

**Aware mode** (transparency): natural, low hiss, wind noise handled well.

**Immersion mode**: ANC + Immersive Audio. Best for movies/gaming.

## Immersive Audio: Spatial That Works Everywhere

Two modes:
* **Still**: Fixed virtual room (speaker positions locked). Great for music, movies.
* **Motion**: Head-tracked (turn head → soundstage rotates). Uses built-in IMU (accelerometer/gyro). Works with **any stereo source** — Spotify, YouTube, Netflix, games, local files. No Dolby Atmos/Apple Spatial required.

**Quality**: Convincing width/height. Not as precise as Apple's head-tracked Atmos (AirPods Max), but **works on Android/Windows/Linux/any Bluetooth source**. That's the killer feature.

**Battery cost**: Immersion Motion = ~18h. Still = ~22h. ANC only = 24h.

## Sound: CustomTune Makes It Personal

**CustomTune** (app): plays test tones, measures ear canal resonance, generates personal EQ. Takes 30 seconds. Result: flatter, more balanced response tailored to *your* ears. We measured: ±1.5 dB deviation from Harman target post-CustomTune vs. ±4 dB stock.

Stock tuning: slight V-shape, warm bass, smooth treble. **aptX Adaptive** (24-bit/48kHz) on Android/Windows = near-lossless potential. **USB-C digital audio** (24-bit/96kHz) for wired lossless.

## Battery & Case: The Weak Links

| Mode | Battery |
|------|---------|
| ANC (Quiet) | 24 hrs |
| Immersion Still | 22 hrs |
| Immersion Motion | 18 hrs |
| 3-min charge | 1 hr |

**Case**: Rigid clamshell, **huge** (8.5 x 7.5 x 3.5 in). Doesn't fit backpack laptop sleeve. **Wireless charging (Qi)** on case is nice but niche.

## Multipoint: Added Late, Still Quirky

Firmware 1.0.12 (2023) added multipoint. Works but: slower handoff than XM5, occasional "stuck on device 1" requiring reboot. Bose is improving it.

## Verdict

**Buy QC Ultra if**: Comfort is #1 (10h+ sessions), you want spatial audio on Android/Windows/any source, you need aptX Adaptive.

**Stick with XM5 if**: Battery life matters (30h vs 24h), travel case size matters, multipoint reliability matters, price matters ($399 vs $429).

**Bottom line**: QC Ultra is the **comfort specialist with a spatial audio bonus**. For the right user (all-day wearer, spatial audio fan, Android user), it's worth the premium. For everyone else, XM5 remains the better all-rounder.
