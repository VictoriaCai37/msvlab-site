## The SSD That Sets the Standard

Since launch, the **Samsung 990 Pro** has been the **reference PCIe 4.0 SSD** — the one every competitor (WD SN850X, Crucial T500, Kingston KC3000, Lexar NM790) benchmarks against. The **2TB with heatsink** ($180) adds PS5 certification, thermal headroom, and peace of mind.

We tested the **2TB heatsink model** in: PS5 (primary), desktop PC (secondary), laptop upgrade (tertiary) for 6 months.

## Performance: Numbers That Translate

| Benchmark | 990 Pro 2TB | WD SN850X 2TB | Crucial T500 2TB |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|------------------|
| CrystalDiskMark Seq R/W | 7,412 / 6,885 | 7,340 / 6,600 | 7,200 / 6,500 |
| Random 4K Q32T16 R/W | 1.38M / 1.52M | 1.35M / 1.48M | 1.30M / 1.40M |
| PCMark 10 Storage | 3,420 | 3,350 | 3,280 |
| 3DMark Storage | 3,850 | 3,780 | 3,720 |
| Game Load (Cyberpunk, Steam) | 8.2s | 8.4s | 8.5s |
| Windows Boot (cold) | 9.1s | 9.3s | 9.4s |

**Real world**: indistinguishable from competitors in daily use. The **DRAM cache** (2 GB LPDDR4 on 2TB) shines in sustained writes and mixed workloads — no SLC cache exhaustion slowdowns.

## Thermals: Heatsink Earns Its Keep

**Bare drive**: hits 82°C under sustained write (Chia plotting, 4K video export) → throttles to ~4,500 MB/s.

**With heatsink**: peaks at 67°C → **no throttle**, maintains 6,900 MB/s write. Copper heat pipe + graphene thermal pad + aluminum fins = 15°C drop.

**PS5 requirement**: Sony mandates heatsink + single-sided design. 990 Pro heatsink fits **perfectly under the cover**, no throttle in hours of Ratchet & Clank, Spider-Man 2, FFXVI.

| TBW | **1,200** | 800-1,000 |
| Warranty | **5 years** | 5 years |

**1,200 TBW** = write 650 GB/day for 5 years. For consumers, effectively infinite. The **Pascal controller** (Samsung in-house) + **8th-gen V-NAND** have proven field reliability — critical for irreplaceable data.

## Magician Software: Actually Useful

* Health monitoring (SMART, temperature, TBW %)
* Performance benchmark (built-in)
* **Over-provisioning** (allocate 10-25% for sustained performance)
* Secure erase (PSID revert)
* BitLocker / FileVault 2 management
* Firmware update (critical — apply 4B2QGXA7+)

Available on Windows, macOS, Android (for external enclosures).

## The 2023 Firmware Bug: Fixed

Early 2023 firmware (5B2QGXA7) falsely reported "Critical" health at 0% TBW due to SMART attribute bug. **Fixed in 4B2QGXA7 (July 2023)**. All drives sold now have updated firmware. Check in Magician → update if needed.

## Verdict

**PS5**: **Buy 990 Pro 2TB Heatsink.** Official compatibility, fits perfectly, no throttle, 5-year warranty. The $20 premium over SN850X is insurance.

**Desktop PC**: 990 Pro or SN850X — both excellent. 990 Pro edges in sustained writes + Magician software.

**Laptop**: 990 Pro bare (check 2.38mm clearance) or heatsink (3.5mm, check manual). Best power efficiency = best battery life.

**Budget pick**: Lexar NM790 (no DRAM, HMB only) — 90% performance, 70% price.

**Bottom line**: The 990 Pro is the **safe, fast, reliable choice**. In storage, that's everything.
