## The Benchmark That Refuses to Budge

Three years after launch, the **WH-1000XM5** is still the headphone every competitor targets — and most miss. **Dual Processor (V1 + QN1) + 8 microphones** deliver ANC that silences jet engines, train rumble, and office HVAC better than anything else. **30-hour battery**, **multipoint**, **LDAC**, and **250g comfort** complete the package.

We've used the Midnight Blue model daily for 14 months: flights, trains, open office, coffee shops, home.

## ANC: The Low-Frequency Monster

Sony's **V1 processor** handles low frequencies (engine hum, AC rumble) — **up to 35 dB attenuation at 100-300 Hz**. **QN1** handles mids/highs (voices, announcements). Together: **best-in-class broadband ANC**.

Real-world:
* **Airplane (cruise)**: Engine note → near-silence. Cabin pressure changes handled smoothly.
* **Subway**: Screech/rumble → muted to distant hum.
* **Open office**: HVAC + keyboard clatter → gone. Voices at 10ft → faint murmur.
* **Wind noise**: **Auto Wind Noise Reduction** (new in firmware) works surprisingly well.

**Adaptive Sound Control** learns locations (Home, Work, Gym) and auto-adjusts ANC/Ambient. Reliable after 2-3 visits.

## Sound: Consumer Fun, Not Reference

**V-shaped with warm bass**: elevated sub-bass (+6 dB), slight mid recession, treble peak at 8 kHz. Great for pop, hip-hop, EDM, movies. **Not** for mixing/mastering.

**EQ in app**: 5-band + presets + custom. "Bright" preset + -2 dB at 100 Hz = surprisingly neutral. **DSEE Extreme** upscales compressed streams (Spotify, YouTube) — subtle but audible on cymbals/transients.

**360 Reality Audio** (Sony's spatial audio): works with Tidal, Amazon Music, nugs.net. Head-tracking via phone sensors (not built-in). Good, not Dolby Atmos level.

## Battery: 30 Hours Is No Joke

| Usage | Battery Life |
|-------|--------------|
| ANC on, LDAC, 50% vol | 28-30 hrs |
| ANC on, AAC, 50% vol | 30-32 hrs |
| ANC off, AAC, 50% vol | 38-40 hrs |
| 3-min USB-C charge | 1 hr playback |
| Full charge | 3 hrs |

**No wireless charging**. USB-C only.

## Comfort: 8+ Hours Without Fatigue

**250g** (XM4: 254g). **Soft fit leather** (protein leather) distributes pressure. **Stepless slider** (no notches) finds perfect fit. Clamping force: moderate — secure but not vice-like. Glasses wearers: minimal temple pressure.

**Case**: Semi-hard shell, bulky (doesn't fold flat). Fits in backpack laptop sleeve, not coat pocket.

## Multipoint: Actually Seamless

Pair phone + laptop. **Video on laptop → phone rings → audio pauses → answer on headphones → hang up → video resumes.** Works 99% of the time. Rare multipoint bugs (audio stuck on wrong device) fixed in 2023 firmware.

## Verdict

**Buy WH-1000XM5 if**: ANC is #1 priority, you need 30h battery, you use multipoint daily, you want all-day comfort.

**Consider alternatives**: Neutral sound → **Sennheiser Momentum 4** (60h battery, aptX Adaptive). Apple ecosystem → **AirPods Max** (spatial audio, build). Best ANC + aptX → **Bose QC Ultra**.

For **commuters, travelers, open-office workers**: XM5 is still the default choice. It just works.
