## The Thermostat That Breathes With You

Most smart thermostats optimize for **energy savings**. The **Ecobee Smart Thermostat Premium** adds a dimension nobody else does: **air quality**. Built-in sensors track **AQI, VOCs, CO₂, and relative humidity** in real time — displayed on the thermostat and in the app. In wildfire season or during cooking, you *see* the air degrade and can trigger your HVAC fan or air purifier automatically.

## Radar Occupancy: No More "Away" False Positives

Traditional thermostats use PIR motion sensors — if you're reading, working, or watching TV, they think you're gone. Ecobee's **millimeter-wave radar** detects micro-movements (breathing, typing) up to **15 feet**. In our test, it kept "Home" mode during a 2-hour movie marathon where Nest switched to "Away" after 20 minutes.

## Triple Voice Assistant: Pick Your Poison

**Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant** are all built in — no hub, no phone nearby. Say "Hey Siri, set heat to 70" or "Alexa, what's the air quality?" Works even if internet is down (local voice processing for basic commands). Privacy: mic mute switch on the back.

## Matter + Thread: Future-Proof

**Matter over Wi-Fi** for broad compatibility. **Thread border router** built in — extends your Thread mesh for other Matter devices. Works with Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa, SmartThings, Home Assistant.

## Installation: PEK Included

If you have a C-wire: 15-minute swap. If not: the **Power Extender Kit (PEK)** installs at your furnace/air handler in 30 minutes (requires comfort with HVAC wiring). Ecobee's install videos are excellent.

## The Missing Piece: Remote Sensors

Ecobee's superpower has always been **SmartSensors** (temperature + occupancy) for room-level balancing. **They're not included** — $80 for a 2-pack. For a $279 thermostat, this stings. Budget $360 for the complete system.

## Verdict

**Buy if**: Air quality matters (asthma, allergies, pollution, wildfire smoke), you have still-occupancy rooms, you want Siri-native control.

**Skip if**: You just want scheduling + energy reports → **Nest Thermostat ($169)**. You want included room sensors → **Honeywell Home T9 ($199)**.

The Ecobee Premium is a **health device disguised as a thermostat**. For the right household, it's worth every penny.
