## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: WH-1000XM5 vs XM4 — upgrade?**
A: XM5: better ANC (esp. voices), lighter, better mic, USB-C, no fold. XM4: folds smaller, cheaper. If you have XM4, **no**. New buy → **XM5**.

**Q: Do I need aptX Lossless?**
A: Only if you have **Snapdragon Sound phone** (Xiaomi 14, ROG Phone 8, Xperia 1 VI, etc.) and hear compression artifacts on LDAC/AAC. Most people: **LDAC 990kbps is transparent**.

**Q: AirPods Pro 2 on Android?**
A: Works as basic Bluetooth (AAC only). **No** Adaptive Audio, Conversation Awareness, Spatial Audio, auto-switch, Find My. **Don't buy for Android.**

**Q: Sonos Era 300 vs Era 100?**
A: Era 300 = 6 drivers, Atmos spatial, upward/side beamforming, $449. Era 100 = 2 drivers, stereo only, $249. For spatial → **Era 300**. For stereo multiroom → **Era 100**.
