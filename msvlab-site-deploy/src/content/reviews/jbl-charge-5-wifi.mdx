## The Portable/Smart Speaker Hybrid We've Waited For

Portable speakers: Bluetooth only, battery-powered, go anywhere.
Smart speakers: Wi-Fi, voice, multiroom, plugged in.

The **JBL Charge 5 Wi-Fi** says: **Why not both?**

**Wi-Fi at home**: AirPlay 2 (iOS/Mac), Alexa Multi-Room Music (Echo groups), Spotify Connect, Tidal Connect — **phone sleeps, music streams direct from cloud**.

**Bluetooth on the go**: 20h battery, IP67, power bank, PartyBoost.

It's the **only speaker that truly bridges both worlds**.

## Wi-Fi Setup: 2 Minutes

1. Open **JBL One app** → "Add Device"
2. Speaker pulses → app finds it
3. Select Wi-Fi network → enter password
4. Choose services: **AirPlay 2** (auto), **Alexa MRM** (link Amazon), **Spotify Connect** (auto), **Tidal Connect** (auto)
5. Done.

**Switch modes**: Press Wi-Fi/Bluetooth button on speaker. **LED ring**: White = Wi-Fi, Blue = Bluetooth.

## Sound: Classic JBL, Refined

**52mm racetrack woofer + 20mm separate tweeter** (Charge 5 had combined driver). **40W RMS** — louder, clearer separation.

* **Bass**: Punchy, present, not boomy (passive radiators help)
* **Mids**: Vocals clear, slight recession (JBL house curve)
* **Highs**: Tweeter adds sparkle, no harshness
* **Volume**: Gets **loud** — 95 dB @ 1m, distortion &lt;5% at max

**EQ in app**: 5-band + presets (Bass Boost, Vocal, Flat). Persists across Wi-Fi/Bluetooth.

## Battery: The Wi-Fi Tax

| Mode | Battery Life |
|------|--------------|
| Bluetooth, 50% vol | **20 hrs** |
| Wi-Fi, 50% vol | **~12 hrs** |
| Power bank (phone charge) | ~1.5× phone |

Wi-Fi radio + cloud streaming = more power. **Still beats most portable Wi-Fi speakers** (Sonos Roam: 10h, Bose Portable: 12h).

**Power bank**: USB-A out (5V/2A) charges phone while playing. 7,500 mAh ≈ 1.5× iPhone 15.

## PartyBoost: The Party Trick

* **Stereo Pair**: 2× Charge 5 Wi-Fi = true L/R separation
* **Party Mode**: Link **100+** JBL PartyBoost speakers (Charge 5, Flip 6, Xtreme 3, Boombox 3) — all play same audio

Works across Wi-Fi/Bluetooth modes. Great for backyard parties.

## Durability: IP67 = Peace of Mind

**Dustproof**. **Waterproof**: 1 meter, 30 minutes. **Floats** (barely). Sand, beer, pool splash, rain — rinse and go.

## App: Functional, Not Beautiful

JBL One does the job: Wi-Fi setup, EQ, firmware, PartyBoost, Alexa linking, battery %. **Less polished** than Sonos/Bose apps — occasional sync delays, clunky multiroom management. But it works.

## Verdict

**Buy if**: You want **one speaker** for home Wi-Fi streaming + portable Bluetooth + power bank + IP67. Nothing else does all four.

**Skip if**: You only need portable Bluetooth (save $30 → Charge 5 non-Wi-Fi), you're in Sonos ecosystem (Roam integrates better), you need Google Assistant, you want audiophile sound (look at Sonos Move 2).

**Bottom line**: Charge 5 Wi-Fi is the **most versatile speaker under $200**. The Wi-Fi/Bluetooth dual-mode is genuinely useful, not a gimmick. At $179, it's a steal.
