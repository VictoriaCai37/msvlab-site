## The Audiophile TWS, Finally Fully Realized

Sennheiser's Momentum TW series has always chased **wired IEM sound in true wireless**. The **TW4** gets closest yet: **7mm TrueResponse driver**, **aptX Lossless** (CD-quality Bluetooth), **neutral tuning**, **30h ANC battery**. It's the **best-sounding TWS on the market** — if you accept the trade-offs.

We tested Black Copper for 6 weeks: critical listening, commute, travel, calls.

## Sound: The Reason to Buy

**7mm TrueResponse**: angled acoustic port directs sound into ear canal, minimizes internal resonance. **Neutral tuning** (close to Harman IE target): slight sub-bass lift (+2 dB), flat mids, smooth treble extension to 20 kHz. **Soundstage**: wider than any TW3, AirPods Pro, Sony WF-1000XM5 — instruments have space, imaging is precise.

**aptX Lossless** (Snapdragon Sound devices only): **16-bit/44.1kHz CD-quality** over Bluetooth. No compression artifacts. On supported phones (Xiaomi 14, ASUS ROG Phone 8, Sony Xperia 1 VI, etc.), it's audible — cymbals shimmer, reverb tails decay naturally, no "Bluetooth haze."

**LDAC** (990 kbps) for non-Snapdragon Android: 95% of the quality.

**5-band parametric EQ** in app (not just presets) — shape to taste.

## ANC: Excellent, Not Class-Leading

**Adaptive Hybrid ANC** (3 mics/bud): matches Sony WF-1000XM5 on low-frequency (engine, rumble). **Slightly better on mid/high** (voices, clatter). **Wind Noise mode** (new) effectively suppresses wind howl — great for cycling/running.

**Transparency**: natural, adjustable blend (0-100%), low self-noise.

## Battery: The Marathon Runner

| Mode | Buds | +Case |
|------|------|-------|
| ANC on | 7.5 hrs | **30 hrs** |
| ANC off | 10 hrs | **42 hrs** |
| 8-min charge | 1 hr | — |

**Class-best ANC-on battery**. Case: USB-C + Qi wireless. **IP54 buds / IPX4 case**.

## The Case: Brick in Your Pocket

**72 x 48 x 32 mm, 65g** — vs. AirPods Pro (45 x 60 x 21 mm, 50g), Sony WF-1000XM5 (70 x 48 x 28 mm, 48g). **Does not fit coin pocket. Heavy in gym shorts.** Trade-off for 30h battery.

## Fit: Large Nozzle, No Wings

**Nozzle diameter: 6.5mm** (AirPods Pro: 5.5mm, Sony: 5.8mm). **4 silicone tips (XS/S/M/L)** — no foam, no wings. **Small ears (concha < 18mm)**: likely shallow fit, seal issues, fatigue. **Medium/large ears**: secure, comfortable for 3-4 hrs.

**Tip**: **SpinFit CP360** or **AZLA SednaEarfit** improve seal/comfort significantly.

## Smart Control App: Feature-Rich

* 5-band parametric EQ + presets
* **Sound Zones**: geo-fenced auto-EQ/ANC (e.g., "Gym" = ANC off + bass boost)
* **Sound Check**: hearing test → personal EQ compensation
* Firmware, multipoint, touch customization, find my buds

## Verdict

**Buy Momentum TW4 if**: Sound quality is #1, you have a Snapdragon Sound phone (aptX Lossless), you need 30h battery, you EQ your music.

**Skip if**: Small ears, pocketability matters, you want spatial audio, you're on iPhone (no aptX/LDAC), $299 stretches budget.

**Alternatives**: **Technics EAH-AZ80** ($249) — 95% sound, LDAC/aptX Adaptive, multipoint, smaller case. **Sony WF-1000XM5** — best ANC, LDAC, better fit for most.

**Bottom line**: Momentum TW4 is the **audiophile's TWS**. It makes compromises (case, fit, price) to deliver the best sound. For the right listener, it's worth every compromise.
